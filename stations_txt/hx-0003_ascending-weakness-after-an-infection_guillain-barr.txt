=== EXAMINER INFORMATION ===
Questions
1. What is the most likely diagnosis, and what other differential diagnoses would you consider?
Answer: The most likely diagnosis is Guillain-Barré syndrome (acute inflammatory demyelinating polyneuropathy), supported by symmetrical ascending motor weakness, distal paraesthesias, and a recent campylobacter-type diarrhoeal illness. Differentials include spinal cord compression, transverse myelitis, cauda equina syndrome, myasthenia gravis, hypokalaemic periodic paralysis, and functional neurological disorder.
2. What are your immediate next steps in terms of monitoring, investigation, and management?
Answer: Immediate priority is respiratory monitoring with regular bedside forced vital capacity (FVC) measurements and monitoring for autonomic instability (telemetry/blood pressure). Key investigations include a lumbar puncture (looking for albuminocytological dissociation), nerve conduction studies/EMG, and basic bloods (electrolytes, CK). Definitive management involves admission (often HDU/ITU level) and immunotherapy with intravenous immunoglobulin (IVIg) or plasma exchange, alongside supportive care and VTE prophylaxis.
